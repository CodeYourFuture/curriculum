Each sprint, there are tasks to perform (which are listed in the [Backlog](../backlog) page and on the [Course Portal](https://portal.codeyourfuture.io/)). Each task also has learning objectives associated - what you should be able to do once the task is finished. You can find a collection of the sprint's learning objectives on the [End of Sprint Review page](../success) for each sprint. This is what you're aiming towards. At the end of each sprint you should:
1. Check you've completed all of the tasks listed in the Course Portal
2. Check you can do everything listed in the learning objectives on the [End of Sprint Review page](../success).

This formal structure of having sprints with learning objectives might be new to you. **It is a way to help you evaluate for yourself whether you have achieved the goals of the sprint.** If you have done (and can do) the things listed in the learning objectives, you have gained the skill. If you have not, you have not yet gained the skill, and should keep working at it.

There are deadlines on this course. Some are hard deadlines - missing them means you have to leave the course. Others are soft deadlines - missing the means you need to have a conversation with the education team about your progress.

Every week there is a soft deadline - to complete that sprint. You should have completed this sprint's tasks by the end of Monday. This is the first soft deadline.

The first hard deadline is to enrol as a trainee. You need to enrol as a trainee by week 6 of the course. **If you do not enrol as a trainee by week 6 of this course, you will be removed from it. You can request to restart in the next ITP course.** In order to enrol as a trainee, you will need to have finished all of the tasks in the **Onboarding** and **JavaScript Fundamentals** modules. This includes coming to class every week, doing all of the reading, doing all of the exercises, and having all of your pull requests reviewed and marked as "Complete" by a volunteer.
