On the demo day the Launch Module teams will present their products.

Each team has 15 minutes to present. You must ensure that you can present within the allocated time. After your demo, you will have 5-10 minutes for questions from the audience.

Each demo should include at least:
* An introduction to the problem being solved
* A description of how you solved the problem
* A visual demonstration of what you built
* Some information about how you built the project
* Some information about how you worked together

Use our [presenting guide](/guides/presenting/) to understand what you should consider when building your demo.
