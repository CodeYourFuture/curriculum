Work together to understand the theory from your prep and today's activities. Bring questions about concepts you found difficult or would like to explore further. Everyone contributes to finding and checking answers. You do not need to know the answer before asking.

## Collect and prioritise questions – 5 minutes

Write down questions individually, then share and group related questions. Choose which to discuss first.

## Explore together – 70 minutes

Discuss the questions as a group. Rotate the facilitator role so participants have opportunities to practise. The facilitator invites contributions, helps the group stay focused, checks that everyone can participate and summarises the discussion before handing over.

Use prep materials and notes from today's activities to support your explanations. Ask follow-up questions, compare interpretations and examples, and check your understanding together. Record answers and points that remain uncertain. Volunteers can help the group find resources and ask useful questions while trainees lead the discussion.

## Summarise and identify next steps – 15 minutes

Review what the group learned, record unresolved questions and agree how to follow them up. Reflect briefly on what helped the discussion and facilitation.
