Take a look at a software requirements specification template such as one provided by the [IEEE](https://web.cs.dal.ca/~hawkey/3130/srs_template-ieee.doc). 

How does this differ from your understanding of a product backlog, as used in an agile approach?

Use AI to review your comparison. Ask AI to:
1. Challenge your comparison of an SRS and an agile product backlog and identify anything you may have missed.
2. Generate an example of the same BrightStar requirement represented in a specification and as a backlog item.

Compare the two examples and explain the differences.
