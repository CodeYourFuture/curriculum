Use the notes from your Business Domain Discovery interviews. Each person works as the BA for the domain they interviewed. Your partner is the domain expert. You have already practised personas and user journeys in prep. Keep the artefacts lightweight and focus on what you learned, rather than inventing details or designing a solution.

## Review – 5 minutes

Individually review your discovery notes, the problem, desired outcomes and any gaps or assumptions.

## Persona – 15 minutes

Create one lightweight persona for a person involved in your domain. Include their role, goal and relevant pain points. Mark anything you need to check.

## User journey – 20 minutes

Draw a high-level journey showing the main steps for that persona, including relevant interactions and pain points. Use your notes and identify gaps.

## Business requirement – 10 minutes

Write one high-level business requirement that describes a business need or outcome arising from your discovery. Avoid prescribing a specific feature or solution.

## Validate – 10 minutes

Take turns sharing your artefacts with your domain expert, allowing roughly five minutes for each domain. Ask them to correct inaccuracies, check assumptions and identify anything important you missed. Update your notes or artefacts.
