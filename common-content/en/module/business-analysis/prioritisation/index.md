Now let’s try and apply our knowledge of Agile business analysis in the BrightStar project:

1. Prepare an initial **backlog** for BrightStar using the requirements gathered and refined in previous sprints (5-20 backlog items, depending on the level of detail). You may use Epic, Feature and Story types of backlog items.  Use GitHub projects to document this. Other tools for managing backlogs and Kanban boards include [Jira](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira) and [Azure DevOps](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/devops). Find out which tool the company you are applying to or will work for uses, and familiarise yourself with it.
  _This [GitHub page](https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/creating-projects/creating-a-project) explains more details. You can use the Milestone feature as an Epic._

2. Read about [prioritisation techniques](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/prioritization-framework#:~:text=A%20product%20prioritization%20framework%20is,what%20to%20work%20on%20next.), including RICE, Kano, MoSCoW, Value vs. Effort, Opportunity Scoring, and Cost of Delay. Describe how you would approach **setting priorities** for your backlog items. Consider the information needed, the stakeholders involved, and what you would need to clarify about the team's existing prioritisation approach. _Prioritise understanding of the prioritsation technique you know the company you are applying to or will work for uses._

3. Formulate both a **Definition of Ready** and a **Definition of Done** for the BrightStar product development process

4. Describe how you would prepare and run a **Refinement Session** for your prioritised requirements and what changes you would make in the backlog following this session

5. Suggest **Sprint Goals** for you first BrightStar development sprint

5. Describe the **MVP** for your planned BrightStar development

7. Consider how feedback from your sprint deliverables would affect your prioritisation and goal setting for later sprints
