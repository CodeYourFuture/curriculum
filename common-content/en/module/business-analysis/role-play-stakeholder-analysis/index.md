The BA presents their stakeholder analysis to the team during a Project Kick-Off for the BrightStar case study. Use the stakeholder map prepared during prep.

## Roles

- **BA:** presents the stakeholder analysis.
- **Product Owner:** listens, responds and asks questions.
- **Project Manager:** listens, responds and asks questions.
- **Finance Manager:** listens, responds and asks questions.
- **Head of Marketing, Head of IT and others:** participate as required.

## Activity

Divide into groups and assign roles appropriate to your group and the scenario. The BA presents their analysis. The other participants respond and ask questions from the perspectives of their roles. Discuss the analysis and give feedback using the notes below. Switch roles so that everyone has an opportunity to act as the BA and present their own work.

## Feedback guidance

- **BA:** presentation skills, correct use of stakeholder map notation, completeness of the stakeholder list, plan for approaching relevant individuals, and awareness of time and resource constraints.
- **Product Owner:** challenge an incomplete list, demonstrate awareness of the communications plan and ask about priorities.
- **Project Manager:** challenge the analysis from the perspective of planning and time and resource constraints.
- **Finance Manager:** consider whether all relevant Finance stakeholders are included, especially those involved in returns and exchanges. Ask how long the analysis will take and how much it will cost.
- **Head of Marketing:** consider whether all relevant Marketing stakeholders are included, especially those involved in the loyalty program.
