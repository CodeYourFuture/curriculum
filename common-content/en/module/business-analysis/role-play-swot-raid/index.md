The BA presents either their SWOT or RAID analysis to the team during a Project Kick-Off for the BrightStar case study. Use your prepared analysis; Sprint 1 prep includes a SWOT analysis.

## Roles

- **BA:** presents the analysis.
- **Product Owner:** listens, responds and asks questions.
- **Project Manager:** listens, responds and asks questions.
- **Finance Manager:** listens, responds and asks questions.
- **Head of Marketing, Head of IT and others:** participate as required.

## Activity

Divide into groups and assign roles appropriate to your group and the scenario. The BA presents their analysis The other participants respond and ask questions from the perspectives of their roles. Discuss the analysis and give feedback using the notes below. Switch roles so that everyone has an opportunity to act as the BA and present their own work.

## Feedback guidance

- **BA:** presentation skills, correctness of notation, relevance and validation of content, and response to feedback.
- **Other roles:** challenge unclear points, ask relevant questions and consider the credibility of answers and knowledge of the domain.
