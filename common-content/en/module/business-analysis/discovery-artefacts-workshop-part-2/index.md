## Business requirement

Set a timer for {{<timer>}}30{{</timer>}}.


Write one high-level business requirement that describes a business need or outcome arising from your discovery. Avoid prescribing a specific feature or solution.

## Validate

Allow 30 minutes for this activity.

Take turns sharing your artefacts with your domain expert, allowing roughly 15 minutes for each domain. Ask them to correct inaccuracies, check assumptions and identify anything important you missed. Update your notes or artefacts.
