### Recognise Your Existing Skills

Think about your experience during the Intro to Programming course, at work, or in everyday life.
1. Have you ever had to figure out why something wasn't working, even when the cause wasn't obvious? How did you approach it?
2. Have you ever had to break a complicated task or problem into smaller parts? How did that help you?
3. Have you ever made a change that unexpectedly affected something else? How did you identify the connections?
4. Have you ever had to learn something completely unfamiliar to complete a task? How did you approach learning it?
5. Have you ever received a request or instruction that wasn't clear? What questions did you ask to understand what was actually needed?
6. Have you ever faced a problem where your first solution didn't work? How did you find an alternative?
7. Have you ever questioned why something was done in a particular way? What did you discover by asking questions?
8. Have you ever needed to understand how a decision would affect different people, processes, or systems? How did you approach it?

Looking back at your answers, what skills did you use? How might these skills help you as a Business Analyst?
