Learn about formulating a clear vision and goals for a project or product when faced with the assorted needs and desires of a group of stakeholders.  [This resource](https://agile.appliedframeworks.com/applied-frameworks-agile-blog/why-the-product-goal-is-not-the-product-vision-in-scrum) might help you understand the differences.

Learn how to define measurable business goals using Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). Read [OKR vs KPI](https://agilemania.com/difference-between-okr-vs-kpi) to understand the differences and how they can be used together. For additional examples, see [OKR vs KPI: Differences, Examples, and Use Cases](https://asana.com/resources/okr-vs-kpi).

Using the BrightStar case study:

1. Identify one business goal and define an Objective with two measurable Key Results.
2. Suggest two relevant KPIs to monitor business performance.
3. Explain how your proposed OKRs and KPIs relate to the business goal.
