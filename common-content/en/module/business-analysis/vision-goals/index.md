Learn about formulating a clear vision and goals for a project or product when faced with the assorted needs and desires of a group of stakeholders. [This resource](https://agile.appliedframeworks.com/applied-frameworks-agile-blog/why-the-product-goal-is-not-the-product-vision-in-scrum) might help you understand the differences.
1. Explain the difference between a product vision and product goals in your own words.
2. Suggest a product vision and goals for BrightStar based on the case study information.

Use AI to review your vision and goals. Ask AI to challenge your proposed vision and goals and identify:
1. Goals that do not support the vision.
2. Vague or unclear goals.
3. Suggestions that are not grounded in the BrightStar project information.

Review the AI feedback and decide which suggestions are valid.
