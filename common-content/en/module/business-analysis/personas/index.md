Learn about the use of personas - [article 1](https://www.getfishtank.com/insights/personas-in-agile-business-analysis) and [article 2](https://www.productplan.com/glossary/persona/)
1. Identify the relevant demographic information from the BrightStar case study.
2. Create at least two personas describing users of BrightStar services.
  
Use AI to review your personas. Ask AI to review your BrightStar personas and identify:
1. Assumptions that are not supported by the case study information.
2. Personas that are too similar to each other.
3. Important user needs, behaviours, or pain points you may have missed.

Review the AI feedback and decide which suggestions are valid.
