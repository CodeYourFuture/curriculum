The BA conducts a user research method, such as an interview, with one or more stakeholders in the BrightStar case study. Use your preparation to choose a method appropriate to the situation.

## Roles

- **BA:** conducts the research, for example as an interviewer.
- **Interviewees or other research participants:** take the roles of Finance Manager, customer services, cashier or other relevant stakeholders.

## Activity

Divide into groups and assign roles appropriate to your group and the chosen method. The BA conducts the research with the participants, who respond from the perspectives of their roles. Discuss the experience and give feedback using the notes below. Switch roles so that everyone has an opportunity to act as the BA.

## Feedback guidance

- **BA:** presentation skills, relevance of questions, listening skills, follow-up questions and completeness of scope.
- **Interviewees:** relevance and credibility of answers, understanding of the domain and clarity of answers.
